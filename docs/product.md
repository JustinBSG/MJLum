# Product rules

The current version is **Version 1 · Release 1 (1.0.0)**, with UAT accepted. See the [release record](releases/v1.0.0.md) for provenance and scope.

The complete product rules are in [guide.md](guide.md). This entry point does not define or override scoring rules.

MJLum is an in-person Mahjong scorekeeper with a Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) interface. It stores data locally on one device, with no account, ads, or data sharing. The first release supports Hong Kong Mahjong; TV display is second-release work. For that second release, a 麻雀諗 product decision dated 25 November 2026 (Asia/Hong Kong) plans showing Spotify now-playing info (song name, duration, and similar) on the unique AirPlay 現時賽果 screen; playing or remote-controlling Spotify remains unspecified, and later product/policy checks stay open in [guide.md](guide.md) and [development.md](development.md). Settlement shows net balances and the pool, without generating transfer pairings. Interface changes preserve the existing scoring engine and validation.
